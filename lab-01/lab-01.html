Creamy Garlic Parmesan Pasta

A comforting and flavorful pasta dish made with garlic, parmesan, and a rich creamy sauce. It is simple to prepare and perfect for a cozy meal.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti or fettuccine
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in a pot of salted boiling water according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat, then sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the heavy cream and let it gently simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Stir in the Parmesan cheese until it melts and the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  5. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until everything is evenly coated.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper, then garnish with fresh parsley before serving.
